Every week our discovery pipeline sweeps Hugging Face and OpenRouter for new models worth your attention. Here's this week's shortlist — all six are live on the [model radar](/models).

## The flagships

**Llama 4 Scout** — Meta's open-weights MoE with a 10M-token context window. The release of the week, covered in depth [here](/articles/llama-4-scout-lands).

**DeepSeek V4** — reasoning-first MoE posting frontier-class math and code numbers at budget inference prices. Our [deep dive](/articles/inside-deepseek-v4-reasoning-stack) breaks down the pipeline.

## The enterprise play

**Mistral Large 3** — multimodal flagship aimed squarely at enterprise agents, with native document and image understanding and open weights.

## The workhorses

**Qwen3.5 72B Instruct** — the dense model quietly winning multilingual tool-use evaluations. Available on both Hugging Face and OpenRouter.

**GPT-5.2** — OpenAI's latest, now routed through OpenRouter with a 400K window.

**Gemini 3 Flash** — Google's low-latency multimodal option with 1M context, built for high-throughput agent fleets.

## The pattern

Two things stand out this week: context windows are the new headline spec, and every major release now has an inference-economics story attached. The radar will keep scoring both.
